## PAGES / ROUTES
1. / Home: asymmetric hero with headline + search bar (event, city, date), animated marquee, "Happening this week" row, category tiles (Music, Festivals, Sports, Comedy, Culture, Nightlife, Business), featured event spotlight, "How it works" in 3 steps (Find it, Pay with M-Pesa, Scan at the gate), trust strip (verified organizers, instant QR tickets, secure payments), organizer CTA banner, footer.
2. /events: Events listing with live search, filters (category chips, city/country select, price range, date: this week / this month / any), sort (soonest, price low-high, trending), result count, grid/list toggle, skeleton loading state, empty state.
3. /events/:id Event details: hero banner, title, date/time, venue, city, organizer, about, line-up/schedule, location block, FAQ accordion, sticky ticket panel with tiers (Early Bird, Regular, VIP, Group of 4) using QuantityStepper, live subtotal in KES, "sold out" and "selling fast" badges, related events.
4. /checkout 3-step flow with a Stepper: (1) Review tickets, (2) Your details (name, email, phone with +254/+256/+255/+250 selector) with inline validation, (3) Payment: M-Pesa STK push simulation (enter phone, "Check your phone" animated waiting state for ~4s, then success) plus card/Airtel Money as disabled "coming soon" options. Then a confirmation screen with confetti-style CSS animation and the generated ticket.
5. /my-tickets: list of purchased tickets rendered as TicketPass components (ticket-stub design with a QR code placeholder generated from the order id; use the "qrcode.react" package), upcoming/past tabs, empty state.
6. /login and /register: split-screen auth pages with form validation (UI + localStorage mock auth). Role choice on register: Attendee or Organizer.
7. /organizer: protected dashboard (mock): stat cards (tickets sold, revenue in KES, upcoming events), a simple CSS/SVG bar chart (no chart libs), table of the organizer's events, and a "Create event" modal/form (title, category, date, venue, city, tiers, price, capacity, description) that adds to the mock list.
8. * 404 page with a witty on-brand message.
